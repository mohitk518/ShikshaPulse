Model: logistic regression trained on 6,000 synthetic students (AUC 0.747, top-10% list catches 28% of at-risk students). Real accuracy will be measured on anonymised school data in the pilot. CSV columns: student_id, name, class, week, attendance_pct, test_marks, homework_pct, fee_pending_days, weak_topic (optional). Excel (.xlsx): same columns on the first sheet; the Excel reader (SheetJS) is downloaded from cdnjs only when you pick an .xlsx file. Data is processed in your browser and not uploaded anywhere.
